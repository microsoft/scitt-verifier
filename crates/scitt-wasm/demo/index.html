          the artifact it describes, so it cannot tell you the statement
          describes the file you are about to deploy. The CLI does that with
          --artifact.

  • The transparency service's registration policy is trusted, not
          re-checked. A receipt proves the statement was registered. It does
          not prove the service was right to register it.
